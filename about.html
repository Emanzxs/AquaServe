🏛️ About AquaServe

A smart water district management and efficiency monitoring system.

AquaServe was built to close the gap between customers and water district personnel. Instead of walk-in complaints and phone calls that are hard to track, customers can report leaks, low pressure, and other issues digitally — with photos, real-time status tracking, and automated priority handling.

On the operations side, staff get a centralized dashboard to organize, prioritize, and assign field workers to incoming requests, while admins get analytics and reporting tools to identify inefficiencies and plan improvements.

Our goal: faster response times, better resource allocation, and full transparency for every household we serve.

❓ FAQ & Troubleshooting

Common issues and quick fixes — resolve minor problems without submitting a request.

Why is my water pressure low? ▾
Low pressure is often caused by high demand during peak hours (early morning/evening), nearby maintenance work, or a partially closed valve at your meter. Check your main valve is fully open. If the issue persists more than a day, submit a report.
I have no water at all — what should I check first? ▾
First check if neighbors in your area are also affected — this may indicate a scheduled interruption or main line issue. Check your account isn't disconnected for non-payment. If neither applies, submit a "No Water Supply" report — it's automatically marked high priority.
How do I read my water meter? ▾
Your meter displays consumption in cubic meters (m³). Read the black digits left to right; ignore the red decimal digits for billing purposes. Compare readings month to month to spot unusual spikes that might indicate a leak.
My bill seems higher than usual. What could cause this? ▾
Common causes include a running toilet, a hidden leak in your plumbing, seasonal usage increases, or a longer billing period. Check all faucets and toilets for drips. If usage still looks wrong, submit a "Billing Concern" or "Meter Issue" report.
How long does it take to resolve a reported leak? ▾
Major leaks are prioritized as "High" and typically dispatched within 24 hours. Minor leaks and non-urgent issues are generally addressed within 3–5 business days depending on field crew availability.
How can I pay my bill online? ▾
Log in, go to Billing, and click "Pay Now" next to any pending bill. You'll be redirected to a secure checkout page. Once payment is confirmed, a digital receipt is generated automatically.
Can I track the status of my report? ▾
Yes — if you were logged in when you submitted the report, it appears under your account automatically. Save your reference number (e.g. REQ-2026-1234) either way to reference it when contacting support.
